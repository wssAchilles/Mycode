# Phase 21 Viewer x Time Evidence Matrix

## Code-derived questions

1. What is the independent statistical unit for a viewer-clustered sequential DR score when slots share a viewer and may share a time shock?
2. Which viewer, time, and crossed-cell memberships must be bound before a slot contribution can be used by a multiway estimator or cross-fit?
3. Which finite-sample, propensity/support, reward/weight-bound, qHat, and stopping assumptions are required by each candidate family, and which are absent from the current Node contracts?
4. Can a training split exclude both held-out viewer and held-out time clusters without leaking the crossed cell, and why is the existing decision-id fold insufficient?
5. How can revealed Phase 20 synthetic evidence be kept separate from a future sealed qualification domain without creating candidate applicability or production capability?

## Decision

No source supports selecting a candidate estimator under the repository's current
viewer/time, support, qHat, and stopping evidence. Phase 21 therefore implements
only a private, candidate-neutral evidence envelope, a fail-closed cluster-aware
cross-fit audit, and a same-process no-candidate handoff. It does not implement an
estimator, confidence interval, p-value, qualification seed/root, or production API.

The paper guarantees are not evidence that current viewer/time records satisfy their hypotheses. The independent count is `G`, not slot count; common shocks must be modeled or cause abstention. The next highest-value experiment is a pre-registered held-out viewer/time replay binding cluster membership, support/propensity and prefix-weight envelopes, qHat predictability/cross-fit digests, and optional-stopping coverage under common-shock stress.
